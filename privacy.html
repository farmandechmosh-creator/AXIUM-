Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026 · Applies to AXIUM web app

1. What we collect

Nothing. AXIUM has no servers, no accounts, no analytics, no cookies and no tracking of any kind.

2. Where your data lives

All data (tasks, notes, focus minutes, energy, settings) is stored only in your browser's local storage on your device. It never leaves it.

3. Notifications

Reminders and coach check-ins are generated locally by your browser. No push service and no third party is involved.

4. Your control

Settings gives you: Export my data (JSON), Restore real data, and Clear all data (permanent deletion). One tap, immediate, irreversible by design.

5. Offline

Once installed, AXIUM works fully offline. No network permission is required for core features.

6. Contact

Questions: open an issue on the public GitHub repository of AXIUM.
