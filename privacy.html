Privacy & your data

Updated Oct 2026 · Applies to the AXIUM web app

1. What AXIUM collects

AXIUM has no servers and collects nothing itself. Your tasks, notes, focus minutes, energy and settings are stored only in your browser's local storage, on this device.

2. Where it lives — and its real risks

Data stays in local storage. It can be removed by you (Settings → Clear all data) or, in some browsers (e.g. iOS/Safari storage cleanup or private mode), by the browser itself. There is no cloud sync between devices. Export a backup regularly (Settings → Export my data).

3. Notifications — honest limits

Reminders and coach check-ins are created locally while AXIUM is open or installed and running. They are not push notifications and may not fire when the tab is closed or the browser is backgrounded, especially on iOS.

4. Third-party services (full transparency)

  • Hosting: GitHub Pages serves the app; the hosting provider keeps standard server logs (e.g. IP). AXIUM adds no analytics or tracking scripts.
  • Weather (optional, Conditions card): calls open-meteo.com for the forecast and, to pick your city, a coarse location lookup (ipwho.is / ipapi.co). No precise GPS is used.
  • Welcome email (optional, only if you create an account): sent via EmailJS.

These are the only network calls. Everything else runs on-device.

5. Your control

Export (JSON), Restore from backup, and Clear all data — all in Settings, immediate, on-device.
